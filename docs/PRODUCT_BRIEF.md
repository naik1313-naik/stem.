# Product brief

## Problem
Students in rural and underserved schools may lack accessible, engaging STEM resources and individualized feedback. Teachers may have limited time to identify each student's specific misconceptions.

## Initial target users
- Primary learner: school student in Classes 6–8.
- Initial subject: Physics.
- Initial topic: Electricity and simple circuits.
- Secondary user: teacher who needs a clear view of student understanding.

## Product promise
Help a student understand one difficult STEM concept through a simple explanation, a hands-on digital activity, targeted hints, and a short assessment. Let a teacher see which concept needs reteaching.

## First vertical slice
1. Choose Electricity and Circuits.
2. Read a level-appropriate explanation.
3. Use a circuit simulation with meaningful controls and feedback.
4. Complete a short quiz with explanations and hints.
5. Save progress and show a teacher a useful misconception summary.

## Non-goals for the first slice
- Covering all four STEM subjects.
- Supporting every grade and regional language.
- Building VR or requiring special hardware.
- Training a foundation model.
- Claiming cloud AI works without internet.

## Impact measures to test
- Pre- and post-lesson quiz score change.
- Percentage of students completing the learning cycle.
- Time to complete the activity.
- Most frequent misconceptions before and after the lesson.
- Student-reported clarity and engagement.

Use a small supervised pilot and report sample size and limitations. Do not claim causal impact from an unvalidated demo.
